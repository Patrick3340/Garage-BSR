Services & repairs

Here is the range of work a general mechanical garage commonly performs in Quebec. Each category groups the most-requested jobs — we always tailor our recommendation to the real condition of your vehicle.

The regular servicing that extends a vehicle’s life and avoids costly repairs.

  • Oil change and oil filter
  • Full multi-point inspection
  • Tune-up
  • Engine air filter and cabin filter
  • Spark plugs and wires
  • Belts and hoses
  • Fluid check and top-up
  • Cooling system flush
  • Wiper blade replacement
  • Chassis lubrication

Your vehicle’s most important system: inspected, measured and repaired to standard.

  • Front and rear brake pads
  • Rotors: replacement or machining
  • Calipers and pistons
  • Wheel cylinders and drums
  • Brake lines and hoses
  • Brake fluid flush and replacement
  • Parking brake and cables
  • ABS system diagnostics
  • Master cylinder and brake booster
  • Free brake inspection with a tire changeover

In Quebec, winter tires are mandatory from December 1 to March 15. Book early in the fall.

  • Seasonal winter / summer changeover
  • New tire sales — all brands
  • Tire storage
  • Mounting and balancing
  • Tire rotation
  • Puncture repair
  • Wheel alignment
  • Tire pressure monitoring (TPMS)
  • Valves and lug nuts
  • Wear inspection and recommendations

Our roads are hard on suspensions. Noises, vibrations or a steering wheel that pulls: we find the cause.

  • Shocks and struts
  • Springs and strut mounts
  • Ball joints
  • Control arms and bushings
  • Sway bar links and stabilizer bar
  • Steering rack and power steering pump
  • Wheel bearings
  • CV joints and boots
  • Noise and vibration correction
  • Alignment after repair

A warning light isn’t always serious — but you need to know why it’s on. Code reading, road test and physical inspection.

  • Fault code reading (OBD-II)
  • Check engine / emissions light
  • Hard-starting diagnosis
  • Timing belt or chain
  • Water pump and thermostat
  • Gaskets and oil leaks
  • Fuel injection and fuel pump
  • Sensors, probes and coils
  • Overheating and cooling system
  • Diesel diagnostics

  • Transmission fluid change
  • Automatic transmission — service and diagnosis
  • Manual transmission
  • Clutch replacement
  • Differential and transfer case
  • Driveshafts and joints
  • Leaks and seals
  • 4×4 and all-wheel-drive systems

January mornings do not forgive a weak battery. Free testing on request.

  • Battery testing and replacement
  • Alternator and charging system
  • Starter
  • Lighting, headlights and signals
  • Wiring harnesses and connectors
  • Fuses and relays
  • Remote starter installation
  • Block heater
  • Electrical fault finding
  • Power windows, locks and accessories

  • A/C system recharge
  • Refrigerant leak detection
  • Compressor, condenser and evaporator
  • Radiator and cooling fan
  • Thermostat and hoses
  • Heater core
  • Blower motor
  • Cabin filter and odours
  • Heated seats and defrost

  • Muffler and resonator
  • Pipes and manifolds
  • Catalytic converter
  • Oxygen sensors
  • Custom welding and repair
  • Hangers and mounts
  • Noise and leak correction

  • General mechanical inspection
  • Pre-purchase used vehicle inspection
  • Pre-road-trip inspection
  • Written inspection report
  • Detailed estimate for insurance
  • SAAQ compliance inspection*
  • Noise and vibration investigation
  • Second opinion on an estimate

* SAAQ-mandated mechanical inspections must be carried out by an accredited agent — to be confirmed against the garage’s accreditations.

Twice a year, a service that changes everything — especially before winter.

  • Winter preparation package
  • Summer preparation package
  • Rustproofing treatment
  • Antifreeze and cooling system test
  • Battery and cold-start check
  • Winter windshield washer fluid
  • Winter wiper blades
  • Remote starter installation

  • Light truck and pickup servicing
  • Commercial and work vehicles
  • Heavy-duty suspension and levelling
  • Hitches and trailer wiring
  • Trailer brakes
  • Scheduled fleet maintenance
  • Light agricultural vehicles
  • Running boards and accessories
