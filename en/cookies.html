Cookie
Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

1. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file a website places on your device. It can remember a preference, make a page work, or — on some sites — follow you from one site to another.

2. Our approach

This site sets no cookies of its own. It is an information site: there is no account, no basket, no newsletter and no browsing analytics. We use no advertising cookies, no targeting cookies and no social-network cookies.

3. What is used

  • Google Maps — the map on the Contact page comes from Google. When it loads, Google may set its own cookies and receive your IP address. It is the only part of the site that can set a cookie.
  • Google Fonts — the typefaces are served by Google. This sets no cookie, but your IP address is visible to Google.
  • GitHub Pages — the host sets no cookie; it records connection data for security.
  • PDF catalogues — the suppliers' documents are hosted on this site and set no cookies.

4. Managing your preferences

Since we set no cookies, there is nothing to refuse on our side. You can still block or clear third-party cookies from your browser:

  • Google Chrome
  • Safari
  • Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge

Blocking third-party cookies may stop the map from appearing on the Contact page. The address and directions remain available another way.

5. Changes

If we ever add a tool that sets cookies, this page will be updated and your consent will be asked for first.
